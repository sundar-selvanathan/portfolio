Feedback from those who've worked with me

Sundar owned product design for a technically demanding product, a poker training platform whose interface has to make dense statistical output legible under time pressure. He redesigned the desktop tracker statistics page, the hand replayer, the HUD editor and the subscription and onboarding flows, and he took the whole product through a responsive rework. Two things stood out. The first is that he worked in the codebase, not only in Figma. He opened and merged his own pull requests, wrote regression tests for the bugs he found, and shipped his designs himself rather than handing off specifications. The second is that he raised problems before being asked. He diagnosed a backend defect in our stat editor and sent the fix, flagged a branch reconciliation risk before it cost us anything, and produced a working interactive prototype of a redesigned statistics page on his own initiative. He also authored the design principles document our engineers still reference for mobile work. He was straightforward to work with, took direct criticism of his designs without friction, and argued his case when he thought I was wrong, which I valued.
